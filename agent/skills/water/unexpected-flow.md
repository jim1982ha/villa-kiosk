# Unexpected flow

enabled: true

## What this watches

Water moving when nobody is using any. Continuous low flow through quiet hours
is the signature of a leak, a failed valve, or a cistern refilling on a loop.
It is the cheapest thing on a property to catch early and among the most
expensive to catch late.

## What it does not cover

Irrigation, pool top-up and any other scheduled draw — unless it is running
outside its own schedule. Without a flow reading of some kind this cannot be
answered at all, and a property that has none should be told that rather than
told everything is fine.

## What to say

When it started, how long it has been going, and how much has passed. If it is
still running, say so plainly and first: this is one of the few things worth
interrupting someone for.
