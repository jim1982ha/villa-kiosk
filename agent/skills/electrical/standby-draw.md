# Standby draw

enabled: true

## What this watches

What the property draws when nobody is using it. Every property has a floor it
settles to overnight; the number itself is meaningless and the CHANGE in it is
not. A floor that climbs over weeks is usually something left switched on, a
failing appliance cycling more than it used to, or a heater quietly holding a
setpoint nobody asked for.

## What it does not cover

Anything while the property is occupied and in use — that is consumption, not
standby, and the two are different questions. It also says nothing about which
device is responsible: it can only say the floor moved, and by how much.

## What to say

Compare the current quiet-hours floor against this asset's own recent history,
never against another property's. Give the figure, the direction, and over what
period. If the change is small enough to be weather or a season, say so rather
than raising it.
