# Left open

enabled: true

## What this watches

Doors, gates and windows left open or unlocked when the property is empty, and
anything still open long after it would normally have been closed.

## What it does not cover

It does not decide whether the property is occupied — it uses whatever the
property already knows about that. And it says nothing about intent: a door
held open during a delivery and a door left open overnight look identical to a
contact sensor, so the useful distinction is duration and time of day.

## What to say

What is open, where, and for how long. Lead with the one that matters most
rather than listing everything.
