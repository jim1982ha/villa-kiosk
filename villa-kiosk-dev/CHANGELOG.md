## 2.552.0

### Fixed — the recipient list could not be scrolled to the end, and its rows were indistinguishable

Opening the list and dragging inside it stopped at the last visible row instead
of carrying on down the dialog, so the entries below the fold were unreachable.
Each row now also shows the id under the name: Home Assistant's friendly names
are not unique, and this property has two called "iPhone 16 Fab" plus the same
phone again as a service — three rows that read as identical. The monthly limit
says what it does in its own label, and the four paragraphs under the AI setting
are one.
